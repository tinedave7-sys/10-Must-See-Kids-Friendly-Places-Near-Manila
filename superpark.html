5. SuperPark McKinley

An indoor play park with trampolines and climbing.

  • Why kids love it: Burning energy while playing games.
  • Best activities: Trampolines, climbing, ball pit
  • Age group: 3 - 12 years
  • Address: The Piazza, Venice Grand Canal Mall, McKinley Hill, Taguig, Metro Manila
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P500 - P1,000
  • Hours: About mall hours, 10AM - 9PM
  • Visit time: 2-3 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, lockers, mall cafes
  • Transport: Grab, taxi, mall shuttle
  • Tip: Wear comfortable clothes and grip socks.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
