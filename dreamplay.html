9. DreamPlay

An indoor DreamWorks-themed play space.

  • Why kids love it: Meeting favorite characters.
  • Best activities: Play zones, dance shows, soft play
  • Age group: 2 - 10 years
  • Address: Asean Avenue corner Roxas Boulevard, Entertainment City, Tambo, Parañaque, Metro Manila
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P1,000 - P1,500
  • Hours: About 10AM - 8PM
  • Visit time: 2-4 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, lockers, food nearby
  • Transport: Grab, taxi
  • Tip: Book early for weekends.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
