6. Museo Pambata

A hands-on children's museum made for play and learning.

  • Why kids love it: Everything is made to be touched.
  • Best activities: Role play, science, arts, culture
  • Age group: 2 - 10 years
  • Address: Roxas Blvd corner South Drive, Ermita, Manila, Philippines
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P150 - P250
  • Hours: About 9AM - 5PM
  • Visit time: 2-3 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, small cafe
  • Transport: LRT-1 Central Terminal, Grab
  • Tip: Ask about workshop schedules first.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
