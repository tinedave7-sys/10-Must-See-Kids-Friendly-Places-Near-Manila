3. Rizal Park (Luneta)

A large public park with gardens, fountains and open lawns.

  • Why kids love it: Space to run and play.
  • Best activities: Picnic, kite flying, fountain, gardens
  • Age group: All ages
  • Address: Luneta, Roxas Blvd, Ermita, Manila
  • Entrance fee (estimate): Free (some attractions charge)
  • Hours: About 5AM - 9PM
  • Visit time: 2-3 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, benches, food kiosks
  • Transport: LRT-1 United Nations, Grab, jeepney
  • Tip: Go early morning or late afternoon.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
