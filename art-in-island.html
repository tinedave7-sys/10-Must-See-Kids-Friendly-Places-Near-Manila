10. Art in Island

A 3D art museum where kids step into the paintings.

  • Why kids love it: Posing inside the pictures.
  • Best activities: 3D painting photos, illusion rooms
  • Age group: 4 years and up
  • Address: Cubao, Quezon City
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P500 - P800
  • Hours: About 10AM - 9PM
  • Visit time: 1-2 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, lockers, elevators
  • Transport: MRT-3 or LRT-2 Cubao, Grab
  • Tip: Bring a charged phone for photos.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
