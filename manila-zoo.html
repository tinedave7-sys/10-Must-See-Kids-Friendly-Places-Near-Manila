2. Manila Zoo

Manila's historic zoo with updated animal habitats.

  • Why kids love it: Spotting elephants, birds and reptiles.
  • Best activities: Animal viewing, picnic, walking
  • Age group: 2 years and up
  • Address: M. Adriatico Street, Malate, Manila 1004, Philippines
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P100 - P300
  • Hours: About 8AM - 5PM
  • Visit time: 2-4 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, benches, snack stalls
  • Transport: LRT-1 Vito Cruz, Grab, jeepney
  • Tip: Bring a hat and water. Go in the morning.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
