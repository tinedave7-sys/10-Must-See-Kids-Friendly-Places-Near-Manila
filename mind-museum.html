7. The Mind Museum

A modern science museum with big interactive exhibits.

  • Why kids love it: Giant dinosaurs and experiments.
  • Best activities: Dinosaurs, space, science shows
  • Age group: 4 years and up
  • Address: J. Y. Campos Park, 3rd Ave, BGC, Taguig
  • Entrance fee (estimate): P700 - P900
  • Hours: About 9AM - 5PM
  • Visit time: 3-4 hours
  • Facilities: Restrooms, cafe, elevators
  • Transport: Grab, taxi, BGC Bus
  • Tip: Book tickets online and arrive early.

Entrance fees and hours may change. Please verify before visiting.
