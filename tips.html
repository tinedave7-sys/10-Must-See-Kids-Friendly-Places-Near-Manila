Travel Tips for Parents

Best time
November to February is cooler. Go on weekday mornings.
Budget
Plan P500 - P1,500 per child for each attraction.
What to bring
Water, snacks, sunscreen, hats, wipes, extra clothes.
Transportation
Grab or taxi is easiest. LRT-1 reaches Ermita and Malate.
Safety
Keep kids in sight and agree on a meeting point.
Stroller-friendly
Rizal Park, Mind Museum, Ocean Park, Museo Pambata.
Food
Mall food courts, or a picnic at Rizal Park.
